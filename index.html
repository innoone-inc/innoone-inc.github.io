Innoplatform · Diagnosis & prediction

Whole-genome interpretation for clinics and biobanks

Analysis, interpretation and reporting of WGS and multi-omics data — for cancer patients today and for the healthy population's lifetime risk.

  • INNOVANOME — multi-omics variant analysis: coding & non-coding, structural and copy-number changes, clonal evolution.
  • INNOPORTAL — clinical data browser and reporting for care teams.
  • INOME — personal genome platform: pharmacogenomic drug-safety card, hereditary risk, lifetime re-analysis.
  • INODE — pseudonymisation and federated analysis: the genome never leaves the institution.
Innodrug · Therapy & drug discovery

From a patient's mutations to the right molecule

AI-predicted protein structures and structure-similarity search turn genomic findings into treatment options and validated targets.

  • INNOVA — drug repurposing and optimal-therapy recommendation by protein-structure similarity, including dose and combination support.
  • INNODAC — antigen / antibody candidate discovery and cleavage-aware target refinement for antibody-drug conjugates (ADC).
  • Validated on 13 metalloprotease substrates · published in Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio), 2026.

One blood draw, a lifetime of safer prescriptions

Health systems already pay for single-gene answers — DPYD testing before 5-FU chemotherapy is standard in Europe. We deliver the same certainty from the whole genome, for every actionable gene at once.

Tier 1

Drug-safety card

Full pharmacogenomic panel (DPYD, UGT1A1, NUDT15, HLA-B*58:01, CYP2C19, VKORC1 …) — definitive, immediate.

Tier 2

Silent hereditary risks

Familial hypercholesterolaemia, hereditary cancers (BRCA and beyond) — definitive, life-saving.

Tier 3

Family card

Carrier status passed to the next generation — definitive, inherited.

Tier 4

Risk scores & re-analysis

Cancer, dementia and metabolic onset risk scores, re-analysed every year as evidence grows.

Global by design

Data stays where it is. Only the analysis travels. Our pseudonymisation and federated-analysis module (INODE) lets hospitals and biobanks in Europe, the Americas and Asia use INNOONE without exporting a single genome.
