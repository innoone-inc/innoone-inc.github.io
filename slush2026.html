Europe already pays for one gene. We answer from the whole genome.

DPYD testing before 5-FU chemotherapy is standard of care across Europe — a single-gene answer that health systems reimburse because it prevents deaths. INNOONE delivers that certainty for every actionable gene at once: pharmacogenomics, hereditary cancer and cardiovascular risk, carrier status — and re-analyses the same genome as evidence grows.
